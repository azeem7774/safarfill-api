Signing key

Make a new key pair

The keys are made here, in your own browser. Nothing is sent to the server. Paste the private key into Cloudflare (Worker safarfill-api, Settings, Variables and Secrets, type Secret, name RULES_PRIVATE_KEY), then Deploy. Give the public key to the extension. A new key pair means the extension must be updated with the new public key.

Portal switches

Switch a portal off when its page changed and filling is wrong. SafarFill then refuses to fill that portal (for every agency) and shows your message. Works with SafarFill 1.1.x already.
